AI SALES INTELLIGENCE — TECH STACK & REQUIREMENTS
=================================================

PROJECT:
AI Sales Intelligence & Daily Business Newspaper

PURPOSE:
A full-stack system that receives historical and continuously updated sales data,
calculates KPIs, detects important business changes, generates an LLM-powered
daily business summary, displays it in a dashboard, and sends it by email and
optionally WhatsApp.

5. BUSINESS ALERT ENGINE
=================================================

Initially use rule-based Python logic.

Examples:

Revenue drops significantly
Profit margin drops
Product sales suddenly decrease
Costs increase unusually
New customers decrease
A region/product shows unusual growth

Important rule:

Python/SQL calculates the numerical facts FIRST.

The LLM explains those verified facts. It should not be responsible for
calculating important KPIs.

11. LLM / AI LAYER
=================================================

The project DOES need an LLM.

The LLM converts verified business facts into human-readable language.

Example facts:

Revenue: +12%
Profit: -4%
Orders: +8%
Top product: Laptop Pro
Worst product: Keyboard X
Best region: Maharashtra
Margin change: -3.2%

Possible output:

"Today's revenue increased by 12%, mainly driven by Laptop Pro sales.
However, profit declined by 4% because higher discounting reduced margins.
Maharashtra was the strongest-growing region. Management should review
discount levels and investigate the decline in Keyboard X."

Possible providers:

OpenAI
Google Gemini
Mistral
Anthropic

Use ONE provider initially.

26. CORE DESIGN PRINCIPLE
=================================================

DATA -> FACTS -> INSIGHTS -> EXPLANATION -> DELIVERY

NOT:

DATA -> LLM -> RANDOM ANSWER

Python and SQL calculate trustworthy business facts.

The alert engine detects meaningful changes.

The LLM converts those verified facts into a clear Daily Business Newspaper.

This makes the system more reliable, explainable and useful than a dashboard
that only displays graphs.
